# StudyOS V30 — Science Evidence & Huberman Audit

Reviewed: 2026-07-22  
Product scope: learning, focus, habit execution, self-observation, and study continuity  
Safety scope: educational only; StudyOS does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or calculate a biological readiness state.

## Executive decision

Huberman Lab was used as a **discovery map**, not as the final evidence standard. The topic pages and relevant transcripts helped identify candidate ideas. A candidate became a StudyOS default only when its practical claim was supported by primary human studies, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, or authoritative public-health guidance and could be implemented without turning a correlational brain story into a teaching rule.

StudyOS V30 therefore centers the observable learning loop:

1. Attempt or retrieve before opening the source.
2. Compare against a trustworthy answer, standard, worked example, or expert response.
3. Classify the specific gap and write the correction.
4. Retrieve again after a performance-responsive delay.
5. Test transfer in another problem or real context.

Neuroplasticity explains **why experience can change nervous-system function**; it does not, by itself, tell a learner which timer, supplement, breathing pattern, or study technique is best. StudyOS selects methods using delayed retrieval, transfer, accuracy, error correction, and calibration.

## Important exclusions

These ideas are not turned into default prompts, scores, streaks, or requirements:

- **Fixed 21-day habit programs.** Habit automaticity develops at different rates across people, behaviors, and contexts.
- **A universal 90-minute focus cycle.** Ninety minutes remains a user choice, not a biological prescription.
- **Cold exposure, deliberate adrenaline, or stimulants after study to “lock in” memory.** This translation is indirect and adds avoidable burden or risk.
- **Dopamine stacking or a biological willpower tank.** StudyOS does not infer neurotransmitter state. Large preregistered tests challenge simple ego-depletion scheduling rules: [23-lab replication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27474142/) and [36-lab replication](https://carlsonschool.umn.edu/sites/carlsonschool.umn.edu/files/2026-01/vohs-et-al-2021-psci-ego-depletion.pdf).
- **Supplements, psychedelics, hyperventilation, breath holds, and longevity protocols.** These are outside an educational operating system and raise medical, legal, and individual-risk questions.
- **Learning styles, personality-based brain limits, or neurogenesis scores.** Neuromyths remain common in education, and biological findings rarely specify an instructional method: [neuromyth systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7835631/).
- **Mindset slogans as an academic intervention.** V30 uses process-specific feedback; it does not promise that a slogan changes performance. Higher-quality estimates of stand-alone growth-mindset interventions are very small or unreliable: [meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36326645/).

## V30 implementation decisions

- Added **Study → Science Coach** as a new tab; no existing page or feature was removed.
- Added a full Learning Cycle with optional readiness context, adjustable block length, pretest, confidence before feedback, closed-book result, gap classification, correction, resume note, confidence after feedback, and follow-up gap.
- Connected Science Coach to Focus Mode, Time Log learning domains, canonical Tasks, commitment intelligence, backup/import/reset, and runtime diagnostics.
- Upgraded Flashcards from ambiguous Hard/Medium/Easy fixed starts to Again/Hard/Good/Easy performance-based intervals.
- Fixed Focused Review so it actually uses one category; Mixed Review now actively avoids same-category runs when possible.
- Preserved legacy calibration meaning for old review records while applying the corrected meaning to V30 records.
- Added an in-app evidence register with claim grades, plain-language use, boundary conditions, and direct source links.
- Added executable diagnostics for evidence shape, safe source URLs, readiness boundaries, SRS planning, category mixing, legacy compatibility, Learning Cycle schema, Task referential integrity, and a disposable end-to-end Science Cycle → Retrieval Task → Planner workflow.

## Maintenance rule

Every future “brain-based” feature should state:

1. the behavioral learning outcome it is meant to improve;
2. the population and task for which evidence exists;
3. the boundary or uncertainty;
4. whether the feature is a default, an option, or excluded;
5. a direct source trail; and
6. a diagnostic that proves its software connection, not merely its presence on screen.
